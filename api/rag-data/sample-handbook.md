# Acme Hackathon Co. — Employee Handbook (sample)

This is sample data for the RAG demo. Replace it with your own files and run `npm run rag:ingest`.

## Working hours

Regular working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.
Employees may start between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM as long as they complete 8 hours of work.
Overtime must be approved in advance by your team lead and is paid at 125% of the hourly rate.

## Leave policy

Every regular employee gets 15 vacation leave days and 10 sick leave days per year.
Vacation leave must be filed at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal.
Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
Unused vacation leave (up to 5 days) can be carried over to the next year; the rest expires on December 31.
Employees also get 1 birthday leave, to be used within the month of their birthday.

## Remote work

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week after finishing their 3-month probation.
Remote days must be logged in the team calendar by Friday of the previous week.
The company gives a monthly internet allowance of PHP 1,500 to employees who work remotely.

## Equipment

New employees receive a laptop, a monitor and a headset on their first day.
Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
Personal software may not be installed on company laptops without IT approval.

## Expenses and reimbursements

Work-related expenses are reimbursed within 2 payroll cycles after the receipt is submitted.
Meals during overtime of 3 hours or more are reimbursable up to PHP 300 per meal.
Taxi or ride-hailing fares are reimbursable only for trips after 10:00 PM or for client meetings.

## Code of conduct

Treat colleagues, clients and partners with respect. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.
Report concerns to HR at hr@acme.example or through the anonymous form on the HR portal.
Confidential company information must not be shared outside the company.
